# FlipFirst Pilot Tester Terms — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

*Draft for legal review. Sent with the invitation; the tester confirms by
replying "I agree".*

1. **What the pilot is.** A free, invite-only test of FlipFirst in the
   Tuscaloosa area (150 miles, $2,000–$15,000 vehicles), for about [4–6]
   weeks. No payment is requested.
2. **It's a test.** Features may change, break or stop. Listings may be few,
   late or wrong; values are estimates from asking prices, not completed
   sales; nothing is a guaranteed deal. Inspect every car and title yourself.
3. **Feedback.** We'll ask a few questions each week and may contact you
   about deals you act on. You let us use your feedback to improve
   FlipFirst, without paying for it.
4. **What we measure.** Counts of sign-ins, deal actions (saved, contacted,
   passed…) and alerts — reported in aggregate, without names.
5. **Keep it to yourself.** Use listings and values for your own buying
   only; don't share screenshots or listing data publicly. Some data
   providers allow use only for evaluating vehicles. **[LEGAL REVIEW]**
   whether to make this a confidentiality obligation.
6. **Leaving.** You can leave any time and ask for your account to be
   deleted; we can end the pilot or your access any time.
