# FlipFirst Seller Listing Terms — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

*Draft for legal review. Shown in the Sell flow before the confirmations.*

By listing a vehicle you confirm, and the app records with the time:

1. **You may sell it.** You own the vehicle or are authorized by its owner.
2. **The details are accurate** to the best of your knowledge, including
   title status (clean, rebuilt, salvage, other), mileage and known
   mechanical or cosmetic problems.
3. **Your contact may be shown** to signed-in buyers on paid plans,
   including pilot testers FlipFirst gives a plan to at no charge.
   **[LEGAL REVIEW]**

How listing works:

- FlipFirst confirms that you control your contact email. It does **not**
  verify the vehicle, its title, ownership or condition; buyers see your
  details labeled "seller-reported".
- Your listing is valued against comparable vehicles; buyers see that
  estimate.
- Listings hide after 30 days unless you confirm the car is still for sale.
  You can change the price or remove the listing any time; removal deletes
  it and everything derived from it.
- Listing is free during the pilot.
- Dealers and wholesalers approved by FlipFirst may upload their inventory
  as a file. The three confirmations above apply to every vehicle in it,
  and their listings are labeled with the business name. **[LEGAL REVIEW]**
  whether dealer advertising rules (price disclosures, fees, the Buyers
  Guide) apply to these listings.
- FlipFirst may remove a listing that looks inaccurate, fraudulent or
  unlawful, or on a credible report from a buyer.

Not allowed: vehicles you can't legally sell; misstated title or mileage;
listings for someone else without their authority; contact details that are
not yours; payment requests before a buyer sees the car.

You and the buyer handle the sale, payment, title transfer and any required
disclosures; FlipFirst is not a party to it. **[LEGAL REVIEW]** Alabama
title-transfer and disclosure obligations to mention; license of listing
content to FlipFirst for display; liability and indemnity.
